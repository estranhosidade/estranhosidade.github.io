From “Turnabout” to “Moglie e Marito”

14/02/2024 estranhosidade Deixe um comentário


I always think about how the stuff that happens in the real world (social changes, for instance) affect the stories people tell [¹] [²‬] [³], as well as what can/could happen in such stories. To give an example of what I mean: how does the fact societies in the last decades, in many countries, become more supportive towards trans people interacts with body swap stories and with what takes place in such stories?

Body swap is impossible (for now!), but assuming it happened. And assuming the new body didn’t somehow magically morph and adapted that person’s — for the lack of a better world — “soul” into that new organism, so that they would end up feeling comfortable in it.

Assuming that this didn’t happen, gender dysphoria would most likely be a factor, especially if the body swap was permanent or lasted several years, such as in the case of the TV show “Lalola” and in other few media such as the manga “Shishunki Bitter Change”. This aspect is usually not addressed in most of the MTF mainstream content because those tend to be focused on either comedy or temporary swaps.

With that said, take a body swap story, such as the movie “Turnabout” from the 1940s, where the husband and the wife swap bodies – the idea of people transitioning were much more unthinkable back then compared to now (paperwork, social prejudice, medical limitations, etc, etc…). So even if the characters suffered from gender dysphoria and wanted to transition, to take hormones and try to adapt their new bodies into being like their old selves…. it would be something much harder and complex. It would be a way more unrealistic path for a story to take.

Now compare this to the movie “Moglie e Marito”, an Italian film from 2017 which sorta tells the same story but almost 100 years later, wife and husband swapping places. If the swap was permanent, the prospect of the couple transitioning seems slightly more possible in a story happening nowadays. Like, you hear news about transgender couples [¹] [²], including cases where both were married to each other and both transitioned.

I think what I’m trying to say here is how can we see the perspective of a character who was subjected to body swap struggling with his gender identity and with a gender dysphoria induced by a supernatural event?

And how a world where society is more supportive towards transgenders influence body swap stories. For instance, maybe there are more stories involving the concept of a cisgender woman, swapping bodies with a man and then transitioning back to her original gender and, in this case, becoming a trans woman since this is more socially acceptable now than in the 1940s.
